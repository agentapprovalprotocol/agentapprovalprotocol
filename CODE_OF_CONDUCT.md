# Code of conduct

We want AAP to be a welcoming project for everyone who uses it, builds with it or contributes to it, regardless of background, identity or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent, especially when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and people working in a second language.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and the effect of your words.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Insults, personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, the AAP Discord server and events where someone represents the project. It also applies elsewhere when the conduct affects people's safety or participation in the project.

## Reporting

Email [conduct@agentapprovalprotocol.io](mailto:conduct@agentapprovalprotocol.io) to report a concern. Include what happened, where and when, who was involved and any links or screenshots.

The maintainers will review every report and keep the reporter's identity confidential. A maintainer involved in a report will not take part in handling it. We will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide how to respond based on the impact of the behavior. Responses can include:

1. A private note explaining why the behavior was a problem.
2. A formal warning, with clear expectations for future conduct.
3. A temporary ban from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban from project spaces.

The maintainers can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code.
